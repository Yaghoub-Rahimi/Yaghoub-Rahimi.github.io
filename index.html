About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at Louisiana State University. I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Georgia Institute of Technology in 2025, where I was advised by Michael T. Lacey.

My research lies at the intersection of analytic number theory, discrete harmonic analysis, and additive combinatorics. I study prime numbers and other sparse arithmetic sets using ideas from the Hardy–Littlewood circle method and harmonic analysis. I also work on sparse signal recovery and nonconvex optimization.

Research interests

  • Analytic number theory and the Hardy–Littlewood circle method
  • Discrete harmonic analysis, improving estimates, and sparse bounds
  • Additive problems involving primes, arithmetic progressions, and Gaussian integers
  • Arithmetic questions in restricted-digit and Cantor-type sets
  • Sparse signal recovery and nonconvex optimization
