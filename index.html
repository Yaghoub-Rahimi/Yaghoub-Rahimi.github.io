About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at Louisiana State University. I earned my Ph.D. at Georgia Tech in 2025, advised by Michael T. Lacey.

My current research focuses on analytic number theory, especially primes, additive problems, and restricted-digit sets. I use Fourier-analytic methods and the Hardy–Littlewood circle method, building on my work in discrete harmonic analysis and drawing on ideas from additive combinatorics.
